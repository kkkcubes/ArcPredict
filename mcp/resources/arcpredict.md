# ArcPredict Prediction Market Protocol

ArcPredict is an AI-powered prediction market built on Arc Network.

The protocol provides AI-accessible market intelligence using the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

---

## Markets

Available capabilities:

- View active markets
- View market summaries
- Inspect individual markets
- Analyze market sentiment
- View analytics

---

## Trading

Available capabilities:

- Leaderboard
- Wallet portfolio
- Trading activity

---

## Activity

Available capabilities:

- Recent blockchain events
- Market settlements
- Protocol statistics

---

## AI Capabilities

The AI assistant can combine multiple MCP tools to answer questions such as:

- Which markets are bullish?
- Which traders are most active?
- What happened today?
- Which market has the highest volume?
- Summarize protocol activity.
